An entry for the make.fun bounty **One-Line Roast: The Gadget You
Use Every Day**
(https://make.fun/bounty/u-daring-gecko-25-one-line-roast-the-gadget-you-use-3d6aa610).

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes one roast line — at
most 200 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My smartwatch congratulates me every hour for standing up. I work
> at a standing desk. It has been applauding an empty chair for two
> years.

The smartwatch's hourly stand reminder, hard at work motivating a
piece of furniture.
